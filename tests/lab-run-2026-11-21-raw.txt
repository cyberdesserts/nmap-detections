--- Case 11, proxying to a backend ------------------------------------
Case 11 is case 1 with a proxy_pass added, so one of the two further
conditions CVE-2026-123633 needs is met.

  $ curl -sk https://127.0.0.1:8451/

  Ports 8441 (no proxy) and 8451 (proxying) return the same two states,
